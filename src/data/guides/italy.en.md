## Italy in 2026: a quota system, and that changes everything

Italy differs from Germany, Spain and France on one decisive point: **most of its work visas are governed by an annual numerical quota that is exhausted in minutes.**

That makes timing not an administrative detail but the route itself. Someone who starts assembling a file in March has already missed the entire year.

**What is actually new:**

**The 2026–2028 Flows Decree** was published in the Official Gazette on **15 October 2025** and authorises **497,550 entry permits** across three years: **164,850 for 2026**, **165,850 for 2027** and **166,850 for 2028**. Across the triennium, **230,550** are for non-seasonal employed and self-employed work, and **267,000** for seasonal work in agriculture and tourism.

**The 2026 click days have already passed:** 12 January (seasonal agricultural), 9 February (seasonal tourism), **16 February** (non-seasonal employed work, form B2020) and 18 February (domestic and care work, form A-bis). Submission technically remains open until 31 December 2026, but **quotas are exhausted within the opening minutes**.

**What that means if you are reading this in August 2026:** your realistic entry point is the **2027 cycle**. Pre-registration (*precompilazione*) normally opens the preceding **autumn** — it was 23 October last cycle — with click days in **January or February 2027**. There is enough time to line up an employer and a complete file, provided you start now.

**Or bypass the quotas entirely** through the routes that are not subject to them: digital nomad, investor and elective residence. Those deserve your attention first.

## The main legal routes

### 1) Digital Nomad Visa — Visto per Nomadi Digitali ⭐ outside the quotas

In force since **4 April 2024**, and its decisive advantage is that it is **not subject to the Flows Decree quotas at all** — you apply whenever you are ready, with no click day to wait for.

| Requirement | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| Annual income | **EUR 28,000** (~EUR 2,333 a month) |
| Spouse | **+EUR 11,000** a year |
| Each child | **+EUR 3,500** a year |
| Experience | At least **six months** of remote or freelance work |
| Qualification | Highly skilled worker (degree or equivalent) |
| Health insurance | Cover of at least **EUR 30,000** |
| Accommodation | Lease or title deed in Italy |
| Validity | One year, renewable up to **five** |
| Processing | ~2 months at the consulate; 2–4 months end to end |

After five continuous years you become eligible for **EU long-term residence**.

**Procedural warning:** some consulates require the foreign employer to have been registered and trading for a set period, and to evidence social-security compliance in its own country. Request the document list **from your specific consulate** — variation between posts is wider on this route than on any other.

### 2) Employer-sponsored work under the Flows Decree — Lavoro Subordinato

The mainstream route, and the hardest to time.

**The full sequence:**
1. An **Italian employer** files for a **Nulla Osta** — the work authorisation — through the Interior Ministry's **ALI portal**.
2. They pre-complete the application during the autumn window, then **transmit it the instant the system opens on click day**.
3. On approval the Nulla Osta is issued by the **Sportello Unico per l'Immigrazione**.
4. You apply for the visa at the **Italian consulate** in your country within the stated deadline.
5. After arrival: apply for the **permesso di soggiorno within 8 days**, via a post office and then the **Questura**.

**The overlooked detail that eliminates applications:** files that have not completed every pre-registration stage — including data validation and final save — **cannot be transmitted at all** on click day. An application sitting in "to complete" or "to validate" status is out of the race before it starts.

### 3) Investor Visa ⭐ outside the quotas

| Investment type | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Innovative startup | **EUR 250,000** |
| Established Italian limited company | **EUR 500,000** |
| Philanthropic or cultural project | **EUR 1,000,000** |
| Italian government bonds | **EUR 2,000,000** |

Granted for two years and renewed for three. The procedural advantage is that the **investment is made after the visa is granted**, not before: you first obtain an electronic Nulla Osta, then transfer the funds within three months of entry.

### 4) Elective Residence — Residenza Elettiva ⭐ outside the quotas

For retirees and holders of stable passive income. The indicative minimum is around **EUR 31,000** a year for a single applicant and close to **EUR 38,000** for a couple, with secured accommodation in Italy.

**The constraint:** it permits **no work of any kind**, local or remote. Income must be entirely passive — pension, rent, investment returns.

### 5) Self-employment — Lavoro Autonomo

A real route, but **subject to the quotas** under the Flows Decree, with a much smaller allocation than employed work. Most people considering self-employment find the digital nomad route faster and less risky. Before choosing, compare the [cost of living in Milan](/en/explorer?to=it) against your expected income and review [which occupations are in demand in Italy](/en/jobs?country=it).

## Country-specific requirements

| Item | 🇬🇭 Ghana | 🇰🇪 Kenya | 🇦🇪 UAE | 🇳🇿 New Zealand |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| National (D) visa required in advance | **Yes** | **Yes** | Yes, for long stays | **Yes** |
| Application centre | VFS Global Accra | VFS Global Nairobi | Accredited centre | Consulate / VFS |
| Police certificate | CID Police Clearance | DCI Good Conduct | UAE police | NZ Police Certificate |
| Degree recognition | Dichiarazione di Valore or CIMEA | Dichiarazione di Valore or CIMEA | UAE MOFA + CIMEA | Apostille + CIMEA |
| Is the quota route realistic? | Yes, with an employer | Yes, with an employer | Yes | Rarely — prefer quota-free |
| Residence permit after arrival | Within **8 days** | Within **8 days** | Within **8 days** | Within **8 days** |

### 🇬🇭 Ghana — applying in Accra

- **Application centre:** **VFS Global in Accra** receives Italian visa applications. Book early; national-visa slots are far scarcer than Schengen ones.
- **Police certificate:** **Police Clearance Certificate** from the **CID** at Ghana Police headquarters, international-use format.
- **Legalisation:** notary → **Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Regional Integration** → Italian embassy consular section. Check Ghana's current standing in the **Apostille Convention register** first; sources conflict and the two paths differ by weeks and fees.
- **Academic documents:** Italy requires a **Dichiarazione di Valore** from the consulate, or alternatively a **CIMEA statement of comparability**. CIMEA is usually faster and simpler, but **confirm your consulate accepts it** before paying.
- **Translation:** certified translation into Italian with consular endorsement (*traduzione giurata*).

### 🇰🇪 Kenya — applying in Nairobi

- **Application centre:** **VFS Global Nairobi**.
- **Police certificate:** **Certificate of Good Conduct** from the DCI via **eCitizen**, with fingerprints.
- **Bank statement rules:** **six months**, stamped by the branch, with a formal covering letter stating the balance and account number. On the digital nomad route in particular, the consulate is looking for **regular income** rather than a large one-off balance — your client contracts and invoices matter as much as the statement.
- **Degree recognition:** **KNQA** and **CUE** domestically, then the **Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Diaspora Affairs**, then either the **Dichiarazione di Valore** from the Italian consulate or a **CIMEA** statement.
- Regulated professions — nursing, engineering, teaching — need separate professional recognition from the competent Italian ministry.

### 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates — applying from your country of residence

- **Consular jurisdiction:** your **UAE residence permit must be valid** for long enough beyond your intended travel date, commonly three to six months. Otherwise you are referred to the consulate of your country of nationality.
- **No-objection certificate:** employer letter with job title, salary, start date and consent to travel; self-employed applicants substitute the trade licence.
- **Bank evidence:** **six months** of stamped UAE bank statements plus a salary certificate.
- **Attestation:** **UAE MOFA** then the Italian consulate. Degrees issued inside the UAE need **Ministry of Higher Education** attestation first.
- **A practical advantage:** many UAE residents already work remotely for entities outside the country, which makes the **digital nomad route** a better fit than waiting for a click day.

### 🇳🇿 New Zealand — skipping the quotas is the strategy

- **Short stays:** visa-free for **90 days in any 180**.
- **The hard limit:** unlike Germany, Italy **does not allow visa-free entry to be converted into residence from inside the country**. The national (D) visa must be obtained abroad before travel, with no practical exception.
- **The sensible strategy:** the quota route is not realistic for a New Zealand applicant without an Italian employer committed to the click-day process. The **quota-free routes** — digital nomad, elective residence, investor — are the ones worth pursuing.
- **Apostille:** documents are legalised with a single **apostille** from the **Department of Internal Affairs**, then sworn-translated into Italian.
- **Working Holiday:** available at ages 18–30 under an annual quota, and a good way to test the market before committing.
- **EES:** fully operational at all Schengen borders since **10 April 2026**.

## After arrival: two things not to delay

1. **Residence permit within 8 days:** file at a post office displaying the *Sportello Amico* sign, then attend fingerprinting at the **Questura**. Missing the deadline complicates your legal position.
2. **Tax code (Codice Fiscale):** from the **Agenzia delle Entrate**. Without it you cannot rent, open a bank account or sign an employment contract. Get it in your first week.
